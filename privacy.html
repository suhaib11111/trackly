Trackly

Terms & privacy

Last updated: October 2026

The one rule: belongings, not people

Trackly is made to find your own bag. It is never allowed to secretly follow a person.

  • Only pair trackers you own, in bags you own.
  • Never hide a tracker on another person, in their bag, car or belongings, without their clear consent.
  • Accounts used to track people without consent are closed, and we cooperate with the police when asked.
  • Found a tracker that isn't yours? Report it below. We can see which account it belongs to.

What we store

  • Your email and a securely stored password (handled by Supabase Auth).
  • Your trackers' locations, battery and times, sent by the tracker.
  • Your safe zones, alerts, family members and share links.
  • If you turn on notifications: a notification address for your phone.
  • A daily visit count per page and app error messages, with no names and no locations.

We never sell your data and we don't use advertising trackers. Your phone's own location is used only on your phone (for "My location", Find mode and the left-behind alert) and is not stored.

How long we keep it

  • Locations and alerts: 90 days, then deleted automatically every night.
  • Share links: until they end (1 hour to 3 days).
  • "Remove from my account" deletes that tracker's history, zones, links and family access straight away.

Who can see your bag

  • You.
  • Family members you invite. You always see who has access and can remove them.
  • People you send a live link to, only until the link ends.

Using Trackly

  • GPS and mobile networks are not perfect. Trackly helps you find your bag but can't promise it.
  • In an emergency or theft, contact the police. The report in the app helps you give them the details.
  • Keep your password private. You are responsible for what happens in your account.

Report misuse or a tracker you found

Tell us what happened. If you found a tracker, include the code printed on it (e.g. SB-0001).

Thank you. We'll look into it.
