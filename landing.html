Questions

How long does the battery last?

Several days in normal use. In lost mode it updates every 5 seconds and lasts about one day. The app warns you before it runs out.

Does it work everywhere?

It uses GPS and the mobile network, so it works anywhere with phone signal in Kurdistan and Iraq. Inside buildings it shows the last known spot.

Who can see my bag?

Only you, and the people you share a live link with. Links stop working by themselves.

Do I need to install an app?

No app store needed. Open Trackly in Safari or Chrome and add it to your home screen. It works like a normal app.
